The club

The Summit Standard

Apex FC is a VR football club in We Are Soccer on Meta Quest VR, founded in 2024. We're a brand-new club: one squad across EU and NA, with training nights and friendly matches now — and league football soon.

“One climb, one crest, one club.”

No half climbs. Everyone pulls the rope: starters get minutes, rotation players get development, supporters get a front-row seat. That is the deal.

United

One squad across two regions. Nobody plays alone — not in a final, not on a Tuesday.

Elevated

Weekly training, match reviews and coaching. We climb one step at a time.

All in

Full colours in every lobby. Kits and voice on — you wear the crest or you don't wear it.

Support

Need a hand?

Tickets, claims and the rules that keep the club tidy.

Ticket system

  • 1Open a ticket in the #support-tickets channel on Discord.
  • 2Staff reply within 48 hours — usually much faster.
  • 3Kit claims, role requests and name changes go through tickets too.
  • 4Lost items or match disputes: include a clip if you have one.

Code of conduct

  1. 1.Respect teammates, rivals and referees — always.
  2. 2.No rage quitting. Finish every match you start.
  3. 3.Be in the lobby 10 minutes before kickoff.
  4. 4.Wear club colours in every match.
  5. 5.Keep voice chat clean during matches.
  6. 6.Wear the crest with pride — on and off the pitch.
